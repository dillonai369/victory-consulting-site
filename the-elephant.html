Victory's Differentiators

What separates Victory Consulting

All learning and development firms want to help you achieve the results you're seeking. What separates Victory Consulting is that we are direct with you about the root causes that are either holding you back or the untapped resources and opportunities that allow you to execute at a higher level. The approach we take to accelerating stronger results can be summarized in two areas:

Differentiator One

We capture the Elephant in the Room

Our biggest differentiator is taking on the Elephant in the room — the truth that quietly caps performance when it goes unspoken.

How we define the Elephant

The truth that hasn't been addressed directly, accurately, or soon enough — and it's holding your organization back.

The Elephant takes many forms: a success stopper, an untapped opportunity, a team dynamic, or a cultural strength going unused. Most leaders either know it exists and haven't solved it — or can't see it clearly, because the higher you climb, the less truth you get.

Victory brings the candor and specificity to name it, understand it, and remove it:

  • What your Elephant is
  • Why it exists
  • The impact it's having — and how to fix it
Differentiator Two

We diagnose and co-create

Listening and diagnosing first — NOT prescribing — is central to how we deliver you a clear return on investment. It begins by understanding WHY you're exploring a solution to opportunities and/or pain points.

Once we've proven to you that we have a clear picture of your landscape, your people, and your target outcomes, we then co-create a solution for implementation.

This is what allows us to establish a partnership with you that is rooted in trust. Every engagement we deliver is done in an environment that enables openness, inclusiveness, and candor.
