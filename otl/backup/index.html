On-call backup sign-up

When a facilitator has an emergency and can't lead their sessions, an on-call backup steps in. We need two backups every delivery day, one for each shift. This first ask covers Value 1 only: Sessions 1–4, the 16 weeks from January 19 to May 6, 2027. Pick every shift you'd be willing to cover. Pick as many as you like.

Days you're already teaching are blocked out for you, because no one is on call on a day they facilitate.
