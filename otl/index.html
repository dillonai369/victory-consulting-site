Times and weeks you're never available

Optional

These repeat every week. Each row is one session start time in your time zone. Click a box, or drag down a column, to mark a time you can never start a session. Click a time to mark it on every day.

Each time is when the 90-minute virtual session starts. Your facilitator time adds 15 minutes before and 15 minutes after, so a 10:00 session means you're busy 9:45 to 11:45. Only times when OTL cohorts can meet are listed, and you don't need to lead in your own time zone. The right column shows which business centers can meet at each time.

Weeks of the block

Each block has four delivery weeks, and a cohort meets in the same week of every block. A week marked Not available takes out every standing day in that week.

3

How much you want to take on

Required

Most facilitators carry 12 to 16 cohorts. Each cohort is one session a block, so 16 cohorts is about four sessions a week. Your preferred times in Step 4 follow these limits, so choose them before you pick.

Back-to-back sessions

Back-to-back means your sessions start two hours apart, so one session ends as the next begins.

How should your cohorts be arranged?
4

Your preferred times

Optional

Pick up to 16 preferred times right on the cards. Press Pick times on a card, then choose a start time. Each pick is one cohort: that day, at that time, on all 10 of its dates. To use the same time every week, pick it once and press Same time on Tuesday for all four weeks (the button names that card's day).

Picks on the same standing day must start at least 2 hours apart, because each session takes 15 minutes before, the 90-minute session and 15 minutes after. Times too close to one you picked are greyed out. Picks also follow your limits from Step 3: sessions in one day and sessions in a row.

Every open time you don't pick counts as Available, and we may ask to schedule you there. If you later rule out a date, time or week, any preferred time it touches is removed.

5

Confirm your standing days

Required

These are the standing days you've left open. A cohort placed on one meets on all 10 of its dates, with no substitutes. Keep only the days you can commit to.
