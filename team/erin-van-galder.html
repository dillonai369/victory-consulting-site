Erin Van Galder

Erin Van Galder partners with corporate leaders, executives, and teams to build high-performance mindsets grounded in sustainable well-being. As an Executive Coach and Certified Functional Nutrition Counselor, Erin brings a unique dual perspective to leadership development — addressing both the mindset and energy required to lead effectively in high-demand environments.

With a focus on growth mindsets, accountability, and clear communication, Erin equips professionals to navigate complex workplace dynamics, foster trust, and drive measurable results. Her approach centers on empowering leaders to align their personal well-being with their professional ambition, ensuring long-term resilience and impact.

Outside of her work with clients, Erin leads an active life managing a busy family dynamic — cheering on her kids in competitive dance and year-round swimming — and enjoys spending time traveling with her family and gardening.
