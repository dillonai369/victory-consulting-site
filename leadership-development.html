Do your company leaders inspire a culture of trust, passion, and collaboration? Do employees know they are a significant factor in the success of your organization?

The Cost of Uninspired Teams

Are any of these present in your culture?

According to Gallup poll data, only 20% of employees believe they inspire high levels of trust and collaboration in their workforce. The difference between an inspired team member and one that may not be fully engaged impacts retention and results.

Morale Is Nebulous

Research shows that work productivity can increase by 50–100% when employees are inspired and report feeling connected to the bigger picture.

Siloes Eclipse Collaboration

In the absence of intentional collaboration, employees tend to keep their heads down, stay within departmental lines and fall into siloes, which results in sub-optimal outcomes.

Trust and Alignment Have Ample Head Room

Because leaders have hierarchical authority, they get less feedback. It is incumbent upon them to proactively approach and communicate to ensure team cohesion. Our field work shows that most do not prioritize this behavior.

Our Approach

How Victory elevates leadership

We help leaders get the unfiltered feedback they need to effectively connect, influence and inspire followership with their team. We bring their self-awareness to an unprecedented level, which is the linchpin for unlocking their leadership potential.

Connect

There's seldom an in-between — team members either feel connected to their boss or they do not. We streamline the path to ensure alignment between leader and team members is front and center.

Inspire

Everyone on a team has individual goals, personal agendas, and a need to stand out and look good. If individual goals are unaddressed or not abundantly clear, trust will suffer and misalignment will surface. Our approach to stitching leaders together with their direct reports opens untapped opportunities on many fronts.

Engage

Leaders need to create teams of committed individuals who want to be part of the company's future — and to inspire them to understand what their role is and what value they can contribute. When employees don't know the part they play in the company's future, it creates unrest. We help executives unearth the gaps, identify opportunities and implement measurable solutions.

Programs

Leadership Development Programs

Strengthen leaders, amplify impact, unlock talent. Leaders are the tone-setters and success drivers of your culture, your people and your clients. Victory's programs focus on developing leaders from the inside out by helping participants understand their impact, develop a roadmap, and improve their performance.

Most engagements begin by diagnosing and assessing your situation; then we partner with you to create the right solution to draw out your leaders' strengths and address their gaps, drawing from our library of core leadership programs.

Executive Emergence

Executive Emergence focuses on the retention, recognition, and development of existing and future leaders. Participants engage in multiple group meetings in a cohort curriculum as well as individual coaching sessions throughout, so development is tailored to both the collective group and the individual's growth plan.

  • Develop greater self-awareness, helping participants better understand how they are inherently hardwired.
  • Broaden participant self-awareness and versatility in interactions.
  • Accelerate trust and camaraderie between peers within and beyond the program.

Collaborative Leadership

Leaders of projects or departments often meet to “report out,” yet they seldom discuss how to resolve conflict, break siloes or galvanize staff in a diverse environment. Collaborative Leadership makes collaboration and peer development the central focus, resulting in stronger individual capability and organizational value.

  • A more versatile, capable group of leaders equipped with the confidence and skillsets to execute at a higher level.
  • A psychologically safe environment where vulnerability and transparency are the hallmarks of a high-performance leadership and team culture.
  • Stewardship and relatable peer learning that galvanizes a stronger culture going forward.

Individual Coaching

Individualized coaching makes a deeper investment in the success of those whom an organization recognizes can have lasting value and contribution, yet who have a specific hurdle to overcome to unearth their fullest potential. It allows a deeper development of self-knowledge, self-recognition, and ownership — the baseline for successful, sustainable improvement.

  • Executive coaching for department heads, EVP/SVP/GMs with large and expanding influence, and officer potentials.
  • Confidence & influence coaching and leadership development coaching for mid-managers.
  • Accountability and measured progress throughout and beyond the coaching experience — typically 6–12 months.
