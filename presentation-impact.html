But Why?

What every audience silently asks

People make decisions based on emotion, not logic. When presenters fail to make a visceral connection with their audience, even the best idea, service, or strategy can fall flat.

External audiences — clients, boards, decision makers — ask:

  • Does this presenter bring value right away?
  • Are they confident, genuine and credible?
  • Can they guide the conversation and also listen?
  • Does my gut tell me this is someone I can trust as a partner?

Internal audiences — employees — ask:

  • Am I valued for my contributions?
  • Do I feel part of something important for the company?
  • Are my leaders and colleagues effective communicators?
  • Do I trust what I'm hearing?

When these elements are missing, employees often say nothing. The higher a leader is in an organization, the less feedback they receive. How often do you think an employee will tell a manager, “You really stunk up there,” after a bad presentation? Instead, employees will discuss it among themselves, while the poor manager continues down the path of mediocrity — or worse. When managers and colleagues fail to connect with their teams, trust, loyalty, and performance are at risk, and golden opportunities are left on the table.

Our Approach

Developing successful presenters

With over 30 years of experience working with clients, we've found that effective presenting is not a “nice to have” — it's a “must have.” Your leaders must present themselves in a way that connects with a broad range of audiences, and communicating authentically and clearly is key to differentiating yourself in the marketplace and winning the room.

Authenticity First!

We can teach all the tactical skills and behaviors required to succeed before any group, large or small. However, authenticity must always come first. If an audience doesn't feel a trusted connection with the presenter, leaders won't be followed and opportunities will fall short. We focus on helping every participant lead with who they truly are, not who they think they should be.

Truthful, Specific Feedback

If someone fails to capture their audience's attention, they need to understand where and how to adjust. We provide honest feedback tied to solution-based actions specific to each presenter, ensuring skill adjustments are made without compromising authenticity. We are committed to addressing the elephants in the room and guiding them out!

Visual Evidence Never Lies

We all have blind spots. That's why every participant is videotaped, allowing them to see the true impact they have on others. This boosts self-awareness and creates a roadmap for each presenter to improve and succeed.

Balance Content with Connection

Presenters often focus on communicating the value of their content, and not the value they can bring to their audience. Whether it's articulating a value proposition to clients or delivering an important message to teams, we help participants organize their message to ensure it is clear and makes a genuine connection with their audience.

Programs

Presentation Impact Programs

Connect, inspire, lead with authenticity. How we communicate and present ourselves is what creates the perception that colleagues and clients have about our experience, trustworthiness, knowledge, and competence. Whether attendees are presenting before a prospective or existing client, leading important internal meetings, or training employees, our Presentation Impact programs focus on ensuring every participant makes tangible, measurable improvement in a short period of time.

Presenting with Confidence and Influence (PCI)

PCI helps participants become more comfortable in their own skin while teaching the vital skills for communicating effectively in a public forum. Through video coaching in a group feedback environment, attendees become more self-aware of how to capture an audience's attention, organize clear messages, and better understand how to exude a more confident, likeable presence.

  • Project a humble confidence to make a stronger impact, inside and outside of your organization.
  • Apply verbal and nonverbal techniques that better command a group's attention.
  • Incorporate tools to manage the anxiety that inhibits the ability to communicate comfortably in a public forum.

Executive Impact

Many senior executives unknowingly fall short when it comes to optimizing their presence, approachability, trustworthiness and ability to be positively influential in diverse circles. Executive Impact dramatically increases participants' self-awareness because they are provided the unvarnished truth and immediately adoptable tools on how to convert their gaps into opportunities.

  • Built for senior and C-suite company officers with high-stakes responsibility.
  • Ensure participants' strengths are emphasized and expanded for more consistent, successful outcomes.
  • Develop in a shared learning environment alongside other senior leaders navigating high-pressure responsibilities.

Facilitating for Success (FFS)

“Teaching has not succeeded until learning has been confirmed.” FFS strikes the balance of imparting meaty, audience-centered content in an engaging, highly interactive environment, equipping participants with adult-learning tools to ensure the colleagues they teach are better prepared for future success.

  • Facilitate meetings and workshops that are both engaging and outcome-focused.
  • Present in a manner that transitions the learning environment from passive (or boring) to interactive (and fun).
  • Adapt to learning styles different than their own.
